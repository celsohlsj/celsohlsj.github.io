Research Lines

Scientific Areas

Integrating remote sensing, spatial modelling, and field data to understand forest dynamics across Brazilian biomes.

🔥

Fire Dynamics in Tropical Ecosystems

Fire mapping, burned area analysis, fire probability modelling, and fire impacts on carbon stocks across the Amazon and Cerrado.

🌲

Forest Carbon Cycle & GHG Emissions

Spatio-temporal quantification of emissions from deforestation, fragmentation and degradation, and carbon removals by secondary forest growth.

🛰️

Remote Sensing & Geospatial Technologies

GEE pipelines, LiDAR, and multi-sensor data (MODIS, VIIRS, Landsat, Sentinel, GEDI) for large-scale forest monitoring.

🗺️

Land Use and Land Cover Change

Forest fragmentation, edge effects, and deforestation dynamics across the Amazon, Cerrado, and Atlantic Forest biomes.

🌡️

Climate Change in the Tropics

Rainfall and temperature trend analysis, extreme drought events, and their relationships with forest cover and fire occurrence.

🌱

Forest Restoration

Secondary forests, carbon sequestration potential, biomass recovery modelling, and prioritisation of areas for ecological restoration.

Field Activities

Fieldwork

Forest inventories, biomass data collection, and validation of remote sensing products in intact and fire-degraded areas across the Amazon, Cerrado, and coastal zones.

🌳

Amazon Forest Inventories

Permanent 1-ha plots for fragmentation map validation and carbon stock calibration in intact and fire-degraded forests.

🔥

Post-fire Areas in the Cerrado

Field surveys in burned Cerrado areas of Maranhão for vegetation characterisation and MODIS/VIIRS product validation.

🌊

Mangroves of the Maranhão Coast

Structural and biomass data collection in Amazonian coast mangroves contributing to blue carbon studies.
