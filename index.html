Background

From the mangroves of
São Luís to global science

Born and raised in São Luís, Maranhão, I developed an early fascination with the ecological complexity of tropical ecosystems — particularly the coastal mangroves and the Amazon-Cerrado transition forests that define the landscape of my home state.

That curiosity led me from Environmental Engineering to the Instituto Nacional de Pesquisas Espaciais (INPE), where I completed my M.Sc. and Ph.D. in Remote Sensing, and then to a postdoctoral fellowship at the University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA).

Today I am a researcher at IPAM and permanent faculty at UFMA, a CNPq Research Productivity Fellow (Level C), and an Affiliate Member of the Academia Brasileira de Ciências (ABC). I have published more than 75 scientific articles, with work appearing in Nature, Science, Nature Climate Change, Nature Ecology & Evolution, Nature Communications, Science Advances, and Nature Geoscience, accumulating more than 5,100 citations on Google Scholar.

I am also a member of the Scientific Panel for the Amazon (SPA/SDSN) and have contributed to the 5th National Greenhouse Gas Inventory of Brazil.

Research Areas

Investigating the carbon–climate–
land use nexus

🌿

Forest Carbon Dynamics

Quantifying above- and below-ground biomass stocks, carbon losses from deforestation and fragmentation, and recovery rates in secondary forests across Brazilian biomes using LiDAR, hyperspectral, and optical satellite data.

🔥

Fire Ecology & Emissions

Mapping burned areas and analyzing fire regimes across Amazonia, Cerrado, and Caatinga. Estimating CO₂, CH₄, and N₂O emissions and developing near-real-time detection systems for land managers and government agencies.

🛰️

Remote Sensing & Machine Learning

Developing deep-learning pipelines (Planet NICFI, GEDI, Sentinel) for forest degradation mapping, secondary vegetation age tracking, and multi-sensor data fusion for ecosystem monitoring at continental scales.

🌍

Climate Policy & REDD+

Assessing Brazil's compliance with its Forest Reference Emission Levels, analyzing the effectiveness of protected areas and indigenous territories, and providing scientific evidence for international climate negotiations.

🪴

Secondary Forest Regeneration

Modelling growth trajectories, carbon debt accumulation, and climate benefits of secondary forests — with projections to 2100 under contrasting fire-legacy scenarios using Chapman-Richards growth modelling.

🌊

Coastal & Wetland Ecosystems

Investigating mangrove carbon stocks, sea-level rise vulnerability, and flooded forest dynamics in Eastern Amazonia, including post-fire carbon emission estimates for Amazonian igapó and várzea forests.

Current Projects

Leading science with
real-world impact

Active · 2023–present

YBYRÁ-BR: Spatio-temporal Quantification of CO₂ Emissions and Removals by Brazilian Forests

National-scale effort integrating optical, radar, and LiDAR satellite data with artificial intelligence to disentangle gross emissions (deforestation, fire, degradation) from removals (secondary forest growth), feeding directly into Brazil's Paris Agreement reporting.

Conselho Nacional de Desenvolvimento Científico e Tecnológico (CNPq/MCTI) Principal Investigator
Active · 2024–present

Amazonian BioTechQuilombo: Biodiversity Monitoring in Quilombola Territories

Combining advanced remote sensing with participatory science, mobile technology, and traditional ecological knowledge to support autonomous territorial monitoring and biodiversity conservation in quilombola communities across the Brazilian Amazon.

Amazônia +10 Programme Principal Investigator
Active · 2024–present

Enabling Large-Scale and Climate-Resilient Forest Restoration in the Eastern Amazon

International collaboration focused on large-scale tropical forest restoration in Pará state, maximizing co-benefits for climate, biodiversity, and local communities through advanced spatial modelling and stakeholder co-creation.

Department for Environment, Food & Rural Affairs (DEFRA) / Global Centre on Biodiversity for Climate Collaborator
Active · 2024–present

Resilience of Biodiversity in the Gurupi Biological Reserve — PELD Phase II

Long-term ecological research (PELD network) investigating natural regeneration trajectories after fire and past disturbance, addressing key questions on carbon stocks, fauna, and the role of topography in Amazonian ecosystem resilience.

Conselho Nacional de Desenvolvimento Científico e Tecnológico (CNPq) Coordinator
Active · 2022–present

MAP-Fire: Carbon Balance Platform for Emissions and Secondary Forest Removals in Maranhão

An open online platform translating remote sensing science into accessible dashboards for decision-makers in Maranhão, integrating fire emissions, secondary forest growth, and deforestation data at municipal and property scales.

Fundação de Amparo à Pesquisa do Estado de São Paulo (FAPESP) Collaborator
Active · 2023–present

University of Manchester Collaboration on Forest Fragmentation in the Amazon–Cerrado Transition

Generation of forest fragmentation maps (1990–2021) for the entire Amazon rainforest including the Amazon–Cerrado transition zone, with re-measurement of permanent carbon stock plots to validate satellite estimates.
