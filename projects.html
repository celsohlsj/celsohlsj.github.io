YBYRÁ-BR: Spatio-temporal Quantification of CO₂ Emissions and Removals by Brazilian Forests

Central laboratory project focused on Brazil's forest carbon balance. Uses Google Earth Engine pipelines integrating deforestation, fire, degradation, and secondary forest data (MapBiomas Collection 10.1) across all Brazilian biomes to quantify annual CO₂ emissions and removals.

Resilience of Biodiversity in the Reserva Biológica do Gurupi, Phase II

Long-term ecological research (PELD) monitoring three vegetation cover types — conserved forest, post-pasture regeneration, and post-fire regeneration — combined with topographic variation (plateau and lowland). Addresses recovery trajectories, hydrological conditions, and community participation.

🏛️
Funder: Conselho Nacional de Desenvolvimento Científico e Tecnológico (CNPq)
📍Reserva Biológica do Gurupi, Maranhão, Brazil

Amazonian BioTechQuilombo — Amazonian Biodiversity, Technology Assessment and Knowledge Exchange with Quilombo Communities

Interdisciplinary research integrating Amazonian biodiversity assessment, geospatial technology, and co-production of knowledge with Quilombola communities. Combines remote sensing with participatory approaches to support conservation and territorial management.

🏛️
Funder: Conselho Nacional de Desenvolvimento Científico e Tecnológico (CNPq)

Enabling Large-scale and Climate-resilient Forest Restoration in the Eastern Amazon

Project focused on large-scale forest restoration in Pará State, Brazil — which has a target of restoring 7.4 million hectares by 2036. Guides where restoration maximises benefits for climate, people and biodiversity. Investigates fire threats to the permanence of natural regeneration and co-develops a prioritisation tool with local stakeholders.

🏛️
Funders: Department for Environment, Food & Rural Affairs (DEFRA) / Global Centre on Biodiversity for Climate
🤝
Lead: Lancaster University (Jos Barlow)
📅Since 2024
Member Ongoing

MAP-Fire: A Platform for Carbon Balance Management — Fire Emissions and Secondary Forest Removals for Maranhão State

Develops a monitoring system for fire occurrence probability and the carbon balance between fire emissions and secondary forest carbon removals in Maranhão State. Final product is an open online platform for visualising fire, climate, biomass, deforestation, and CO₂ data at municipal level.

🏛️
Funder: Fundação de Amparo à Pesquisa do Estado de São Paulo (FAPESP)
🤝
Lead: Liana O. Anderson (CEMADEN/INPE)
📅Since 2022

Carbon Balance of CO₂ Emissions for Maranhão State

Provides a comprehensive balance between CO₂ emissions from deforestation, forest fragmentation, and fires, and removals through secondary forest growth for Maranhão State (1985–2020). Integrates state-of-the-art remote sensing products with field data.

🏛️
Funder: Conselho Nacional de Desenvolvimento Científico e Tecnológico (CNPq)
🤝
Lead: Liana O. Anderson (CEMADEN/INPE)
📅Since 2022
Member Ongoing

ARBOLES: Understanding Biodiversity and Resilience of Latin American Forests Based on Functional Traits

Investigates the functional diversity and ecological resilience of Latin American forests using remote sensing and field-based functional trait data. Focuses on how traits mediate ecosystem responses to disturbance and climate change across tropical and subtropical forests.

🏛️
Funder: Fundação de Amparo à Pesquisa do Estado de São Paulo (FAPESP)

Forest Fragmentation Mapping of the Amazon and Amazon–Cerrado Transition Forests

Generated forest fragmentation maps (1990–2021) for the entire Amazon rainforest including the Amazon–Cerrado transition zone. Updated a field database by remeasuring trees in three permanent 1-ha plots, and validated fragmentation maps against field carbon stock estimates.

🏛️
Funders: University of Manchester / Fundação de Apoio ao Ensino, Pesquisa e Extensão (FAPEX)
📅2022–2024

Mapping Wildfires in the Amazon with MODIS and VIIRS Data

Developed and validated burned area mapping methodologies for the Amazon biome using multi-sensor satellite data (MODIS MCD14ML, MOD45, VIIRS). Analysed fire frequency, intensity, and spatial patterns across the Brazilian Amazon.
