🌳 Main Field Campaigns
🌳
Amazon

Forest Inventories in the Amazon — Permanent Plot Network

Measurement and remeasurement of permanent 1-ha plots in intact and fire-degraded Amazon forests across Maranhão and Pará states. Activities include individual tree identification, diameter at breast height (DBH) measurement, height estimation, and crown assessment. Data are used to validate fragmentation maps and calibrate remote sensing-based carbon stock estimates.

Permanent plots AGB · Biomass DBH · Height Carbon stocks
📍Maranhão · Pará · Mato Grosso
🔗
Related project: YBYRÁ-BR · Manchester Fragmentation Project
🔬
PELD · Amazon

Long-term Ecological Research — Reserva Biológica do Gurupi

Monitoring three vegetation cover types — conserved forest, post-pasture regeneration, and post-fire regeneration — in two topographic positions (plateau and lowland). Field campaigns assess vegetation structure, soil properties, carbon stocks, terrestrial fauna, and hydrological conditions. Part of the Brazilian Long-term Ecological Research network (PELD/CNPq).

PELD/CNPq Forest regeneration Soil carbon Fauna
📍Reserva Biológica do Gurupi, western Maranhão
🔗
Related project: Rebio Gurupi Phase II
🔥
Cerrado

Post-fire Surveys in the Cerrado of Maranhão

Field surveys in burned Cerrado areas for characterisation of fire impact on vegetation structure, species composition, and carbon stocks. Data are used to validate MODIS and VIIRS burned area products and to ground-truth fire severity estimates derived from Landsat and Sentinel-2 imagery.

Post-fire Burned area MODIS · VIIRS Fire severity
📍Maranhão — Amazon–Cerrado transition zone
🔗
Related project: MAP-Fire · CO₂ Balance Maranhão
🌊
Coastal · Amazon

Mangrove Inventories — Maranhão Coastal Zone

Structural and biomass data collection in Amazonian coast mangrove forests of Maranhão State. Field measurements include tree height, DBH, root biomass, and sediment carbon sampling. Data contribute to blue carbon estimates and validation of satellite-based mangrove cover change products.

Mangrove Blue carbon Coastal ecology AGB · Sediment C
📍Maranhão Coast — Baía de São Marcos region
🔗
Related publication: Bezerra et al. 2022 · Science
🗺️ Additional Field Activities
🚁

UAV Mapping of Native Forests (Amazon)

Use of unmanned aerial vehicles (UAV/drone) for high-resolution mapping of forest canopy structure, integrating outputs with orbital data for validation of forest cover products.

EMBRAPA · 2020
🔥

Fire Probability Mapping — Pará State

Field validation campaigns supporting a Random Forest fire probability model for Pará State (AUC-ROC = 0.933). Included collection of fire occurrence records and landscape characterisation at sample points across the eastern Amazon.

Pará · 2022–2023
🌿

Babaçu Palm Mapping — Maranhão

Field verification for geospatial mapping of babaçu palm (Attalea speciosa) distribution across Maranhão State, combining satellite imagery with ground-truth points across rural landscapes.

EMBRAPA Cocais · 2014
🌲

Amazonia and Climate Change Field Course (INPA)

40-hour intensive field training at the National Institute for Amazonian Research (INPA), covering forest ecology, climate monitoring, carbon flux measurements, and field sampling protocols in Central Amazonia.

INPA · Manaus · 2017
🦺

Volunteer Conservation Management — Serra do Tombador Nature Reserve

Volunteer work in conservation unit management, including biodiversity monitoring, fire prevention, and environmental education activities in the Serra do Tombador Nature Reserve, Goiás State.

Goiás · 2012–2013
🏞️

Burned Area Validation — Baixada Maranhense Ramsar Site

Field-based validation of burned area mapping using Landsat 8 imagery in the Baixada Maranhense wetland Ramsar site. Characterisation of fire scar extent, soil exposure, and vegetation recovery across wetland habitats.

Maranhão · 2014
